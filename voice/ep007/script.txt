Three hundred Spartans held off a million Persians.
Real... or myth?
Clue one. Herodotus said two point six million Persians. Historians today say about one hundred twenty to three hundred thousand.
Clue two. The Greeks? Not three hundred. Roughly seven thousand.
But wait. They did block the pass.
Two days of Persian attacks failed. Shields locked. Spears out.
So how did it fall?
A local, Ephialtes, told Xerxes about a mountain path.
At night, Persian troops circled behind. The guards were caught by surprise.
Leonidas sent most of the army away. The Spartans and the Thespians stayed.
Verdict: mostly myth.
Not three hundred. Not a million. But a real stand.
