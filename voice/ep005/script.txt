Einstein failed math.
Teachers love this story.
Real... or myth?
Let's check his report card.
Clue one. At twelve, he started teaching himself algebra and geometry.
By fourteen, he said, he'd mastered calculus.
But wait. He did fail an exam.
Eighteen ninety-five. Sixteen years old. The entrance exam for E T H Zurich.
He failed it.
But look closer. He failed the non-science part. The general section.
In math and physics? With distinction.
So what did this bad at math kid do next?
Nineteen oh five. A patent office. Four papers. They changed physics.
Verdict: myth. Einstein did not fail math.
He failed one exam. Not in math.
