# Sticky and shareable: choosing and shaping the idea

Adapted from **wondelai/skills** `made-to-stick` (SUCCESs, after Chip and Dan Heath) and `contagious` (STEPPS, after Jonah
Berger), Wondel.ai, MIT, commit d8d5d75. Rewritten for 30–40 s wordless motivational Shorts.

## 1. SUCCESs: will the viewer get it and remember it?

Score each trait 1–10 for the chosen angle. Target total ≥ 45 / 60; any of Simple, Unexpected, Concrete below 7 means change
the angle before writing scenes.

| Trait | Question for a Short | Channel example that passes | Fails when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Simple** | Can the whole film be said in one sentence a 12-year-old gets? (the Commander's Intent) | "Same 4 hours: one scrolls, one builds." | Two morals; the viewer must decode a metaphor |
| **Unexpected** | Does frame 0 make the viewer predict wrong, then the film proves the other side? (curiosity gap) | "10/10 in practice. Who scores?" and the 4/10 guy scores | The obvious side wins the obvious way |
| **Concrete** | Is every idea a thing you can point at: an object, a number, a place? | FIRED box, RENT $1,240, STEP 31/100 | "Success", "growth", "pressure" shown as glowing light |
| **Credible** | Would a viewer say "yes, that happens"? Internal credibility = vivid true detail, human-scale numbers | Penalty over the bar under pressure; $5 × 365 = $1,825 | Invented stats, impossible physics, a fairy-tale fix |
| **Emotional** | Is there ONE person the viewer becomes (label YOU)? Does it touch identity ("people like me")? | The YOU figure on the stairs | A crowd of anonymous figures; a lecture |
| **Stories** | Challenge, connection or creativity plot with a turn? | Challenge: gets up under the boxes | A list of tips with pictures |

**Curse of knowledge test:** show frame 0 and the 2 s frame to someone who has never heard the idea. If they need you to
explain, the film is not done.

## 2. STEPPS: will they share or rewatch it?

Mark each driver 0 (absent), 1 (weak), 2 (strong). A film needs at least two drivers at 2, and **Emotion must be 2**.

| Driver | What it means in a Short | How this channel does it |
|---|---|---|
| **Social currency** | Sharing it makes the sharer look wise or in the know | A surprising true fact (coal and diamond are both carbon); a twist worth "watch this" |
| **Triggers** | Daily life reminds them of it | Tie the film to a daily moment: the alarm, the scroll, payday, Monday, the gym door |
| **Emotion (high arousal)** | Awe, tension, relief, triumph, anger at unfairness. Sadness alone kills sharing | A held breath before the kick, then the crowd; the boss sitting on the rock |
| **Public** | Something visible they can copy or say | A comment word to post ("LEVER", "DROP IT"), a label people quote |
| **Practical value** | One action they can take today | "Train under it", "plan 5 minutes", "fix it while it's small" |
| **Stories** | The lesson travels inside the story | The moral is never said; the picture carries it |

## 3. Turning an idea into a twist (the competitor's current winning move)

Pick one per vignette:
- **Reveal by camera:** pull back and show what the character could not see (the 1,000 missed arrows behind "Lucky").
- **Counter jump:** a counter crawls, then jumps (BAD IDEAS 2 → 99 → 1 GREAT IDEA; STEP 31 stuck, then 31 → 100).
- **Wrong-looking side wins:** the one who looks lazy, slow or worse wins (the man in the chair fishing; 4/10 in practice).
- **The thing was different:** a box that looked heavy is empty; a monster is a shadow of a sheet of paper.
- **Inner voice turned round:** "Wait... I'm holding it?"

The twist must be **physically true** and set up earlier in the film (the viewer saw him pick up the box himself).

## 4. Titles that pull (from the 176-short ranking)

- A short rule the viewer half-believes, 3–7 words: "Success is earned, never given", "Brick by brick".
- Or a contrast or number: "I have to win vs I need to win", "1% better every day".
- Concrete nouns beat abstract ones: leaks, ships, seed, diamonds, axe.
- Weak: vague promises ("Life has a bigger plan"), "new mindset" phrases, warnings ("Be careful who you trust").
