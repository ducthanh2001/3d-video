Studied 2026-11-07 frame by frame: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/ILZp4n1YzE8 "Start building your future" (31 s, ~431k views).
Private study frames (never upload/publish): `video-ai/out/_ref/simplyanimated_ILZp4n1YzE8/` (t*.jpg + contact-sheet_1fps.jpg).

Its 5 vignettes, each ~6 s, each in a DIFFERENT drawing system:
1. cliff "What People See" → camera pans down to "What People Don't See" (jagged rock, fire below): textured grey 3D-ish rock, stars.
2. 3 stacked rows "93% / 6% / 1% of people" + cricket wicket: THIN white stick figures (one-line limbs, solid round head, no outline,
   no face); 93% holds the ball, 6% facepalms, 1% throws → wicket shatters. Labels in condensed bold grey.
3. "Loser / Winner Mindset": CHUNKY front-view pictograms (grey vertical gradient, light on top; rounded torso block, no neck, round
   head with two light dot eyes). The burden is WRITTEN ON THE OBJECT: barbell = "What if I make mistakes?" vs "I Don't Care"; winner
   squats then lifts it overhead.
4. "In A Week / Month / Year": 3 rows, line-art (grey outline strokes, no fill): square vs ball → shelf → conveyor machine.
5. Overhead crane drops word-blocks (EXERCISE, MEDITATE, BOOKS, GOALS…) into a beaker of "STRESS" liquid; liquid shrinks and
   changes colour red→yellow→green. Line-art machine + glossy coloured word blocks.

Rules this shows:
- Content sits in the central ~60% band; huge black margins; tiny watermark at bottom.
- Meaning lives in WORDS ON OBJECTS (the weight, the blocks, the liquid ARE feelings/habits), not in captions under the scene.
- One cause → effect per vignette, physical and satisfying (throw → shatter, drop → level falls, conveyor → square becomes ball).
- 3 rows for escalation (most / some / few) as often as 2 lanes; rows act SIMULTANEOUSLY and the last row gets the payoff.
- Each vignette switches drawing system (stick / pictogram / line-art machine) → the eye resets every 6 s (retention).
- No outlines on figures; light from above (gradient); glow only on the "active" thing (the ball, the star).

Re-study of the 25M (65JbnU2wZbQ) and 9.8M (J1q70DHZ3ao) sheets, 2026-11-07: their biggest vignettes are tiny STORIES WITH A TWIST,
not static A-vs-B result pictures (which is what our SAME HOURS/TOMORROW drafts were; the user found TOMORROW v7 "too similar"):
suspense counter (hits 0→99, quits at 99, the other hits 100 → rock breaks); the doer FAILS first (belly-flop dives, climbs back,
then a perfect dive); attrition (a crowd pushing boulders labelled TOO HARD / THEY LAUGHED / NO TIME drops out one by one, the
last one reaches the top and the boulder becomes the rising sun); a calendar race (Motivated quits Jan 9, Disciplined reaches Dec 31);
a pile that grows hour by hour (LATER boxes 9 AM → 5 PM: "Later doesn't go away. It stacks."); one deep hole vs many shallow ones → light.

Their NEWER look (Oct–Nov 2026, studied frame by frame on 2026-11-08): real rooms/streets with depth (wall panels, window with
moon/stars, distant layers), practical lights (pendant cone, phone glow on the face, campfire, moon), muted night colours, several
camera angles per vignette (wide → hard cut to a top-down close-up; zoom-out reveals), motion smears on fast moves, snappy
anticipation → hit → settle, white speech bubbles with bold black text. Our test of all six: src/episodes/look/BedroomTest.tsx
(LookTestBW / LookTestMuted, out/look/look-test_*.mp4) — the user still has to choose B&W+gold vs muted colour.

**How to apply:** design our frames in [[style-bw-one-accent]] colours but borrow these structures (words-on-objects, machines,
3 rows, a switch of drawing system per vignette); never copy their specific ideas — see the used-ideas list in [[competitor-format]].
